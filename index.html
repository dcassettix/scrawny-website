The Scrawny standard

Remove until removing more would reduce understanding, control, or capability.

Truth over theater.

The interface may simplify reality. It never manufactures confidence.

Flow over activity.

Optimize accepted useful throughput—not noise, motion, or agent utilization.

Strength where it matters.

Keep endpoints lean, controls explicit, evidence durable, and humans at genuine decisions.
